About the OptiCrop System

OptiCrop is an agricultural advisory platform leveraging predictive machine learning models to help farmers maximize crop productivity through scientific decision support.

Core Recommendation Parameters

Nitrogen (N)

Crucial for vegetative growth, leaf development, and chlorophyll production. High nitrogen yields strong, healthy green leaves.

Phosphorous (P)

Drives root development, flowering, and seed formation. Essential for early stage crop establishment.

Potassium (K)

Improves disease resistance, water utilization efficiency, and crop quality. Critical for heavy feed crops.

Temperature

Dictates enzymatic activity and cellular metabolic rates within the plant, guiding seasonal suitability.

Humidity

Affects transpiration rates, gas exchange, and vulnerability to fungal pathogenetic spread.

Soil pH

Determines soil nutrient availability and solubility. A neutral-to-slight acidic range is standard.

How it Works

Using supervised machine learning, the system correlates the seven parameters against historical records of 22 distinct crop types. Logistic Regression builds linear probability boundaries across this seven-dimensional space, returning the most ecologically aligned recommendation with the lowest probability of crop failure.
